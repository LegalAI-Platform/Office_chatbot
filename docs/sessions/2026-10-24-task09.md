## 5. Differences from the prompt, and why

1. **The baseline checks were not run again** (1): same code as the checks of PR #8 an hour before.
2. **`hold_until` is computed in .NET** (`HoldRules.Until`) and passed to `book_appointment`, not in SQL:
   the same rule decides which times may be offered at all, so it lives in one tested place. SQL still caps a
   replacing hold at the old one's end.
3. **S-04 has a few more arrows** (domain and SQL): «ما وصلش» → a new receipt counts again; the three marks
   correct each other until a refund starts (a person can misclick); a refund may be due before the review
   (the client cancels within 24 hours); `refunded` by any person (P2), never the system.
4. **Urgent offers start about half an hour ahead**: a time the client cannot pay for in 15 minutes is not offered.
5. **Bookings confirmed before 0014** (TASK 08, no hold) accept a receipt as theirs (their payment is `requested`).
6. **A receipt kept as a credit**: from a hold that ran out (the time taken) or a paid booking the office
   cancelled; the next booking of the same service is confirmed at once. Without it, TASK 08's «ميعاد تاني»
   after the office cancelled would have asked the client to pay again.
7. **The reminder** goes only for holds of 45 minutes or more, and says «فاضل نص ساعة».
8. **`booking_confirmed` (1.4)** stays active but is no longer sent; the hold's message replaces it.
9. **The panel's JS reached the old budget** (149.9 / 150 KB): the payments page's texts were shortened to fit.
   Then the owner removed the limit (5b): the build only prints the size.
10. **Booking from WhatsApp waited for the texts** until the owner approved them the same day (5b);
    `booking.enabled` stayed on (the owner's test) throughout.

## 5c. Fixes from the PR #10 review (same day)

1. **A receipt marked «ما وصلش» is never a credit**: before, after the office cancelled, the client's next
   booking of that service was confirmed with no money.
2. **«استرداد كامل» is promised only when there is money to give back**: a press on an old cancellation whose
   receipt already pays another booking (or that was never paid) no longer opens a refund task.
3. **Overpaid then cancelled within 24 hours** refunds everything that came, not only the overpaid difference.
4. **A hold replaced by another time is `cancelled_by_client`**, not `expired`: an expired hold may take a late
   receipt, so a photo sent long after could have confirmed a time the client gave up.
5. **«ناقص» / «ما وصلش» are told to the client only while his booking stands** (never for a cancelled one).
6. **One rule for «what does this receipt pay for»**: `hazem.receipt_target`, used by the API and by
   `attach_receipt` (the C# copy ordered one case differently).
7. Opening a receipt is recorded only once the file was really read; the report's «وصل» leaves out money
   given back; amounts are written by one helper (`BookingWords.Money`).
