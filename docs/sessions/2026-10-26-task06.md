## 4. Differences from the prompt, and why

1. **The prices are settings (`price.*`), not a table `hazem.service_prices`.** Every part already reads the settings each
   turn (the flow, the workers, the SQL through `hazem.price()`), as `booking.urf_minutes` does; the history is the audit
   (`price.changed` with from → to). Same behavior the owner approved, less to keep in step.
2. **The prices go into the texts when the texts are read**, not as a fill in each step: one place, and the workers'
   messages are covered too.
3. **«أونلاين ولا في المكتب؟» has no text of its own**: the consultation offer already says both ways with their prices.
4. **The three relabelled eval samples** (132, 133, 1036) were `other` because no office intent existed; their new labels
   follow the owner's decisions, they were not tuned on.
5. **v7 is not measured** (no `HAZEM_OPENAI_API_KEY` on this machine). The new intent is tested with the stub only.
