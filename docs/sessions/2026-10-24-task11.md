## 4. Differences from the prompt, and why

1. **The documents are asked for after the receipt**, not with the questions: an image while the hold waits is its
   receipt (TASK 09's rule), so ID photos sent before it would be taken as receipts. The list of what is needed is
   still said with the price and the times; a PDF is accepted any time.
2. **`type_unclear` is not stored** (S-06 1.15): «عرفي» alone gets «قولّي المطلوب» and the case starts once the kind is
   known.
3. **The confirmation's attendee list is filled from the answers** (`{{attendees}}`): B-11.7's «الزوج والزوجة والولي
   [والشاهدين]», and for a divorce «الطرفين [والشاهدين]» (from B-11's divorce step 6). The word-for-word test maps the slot.
4. **Approval needs the appointment still on**; refusal works before or after the booking.
5. **Encryption at rest:** Garage (test) does not encrypt; R2 (production, TASK 14) encrypts every object by default. The
   documents are only in `office-sensitive` and opened from the panel, every opening recorded.
