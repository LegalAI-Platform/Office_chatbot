## 5. Differences from the prompt, and why

1. **A listed client's Person is made at their first message** (from the list's name), not at
   import time: same result for B-01.5, and no personal rows for people who never write.
2. **Task types beyond S-05** (`lawyer_direct_message`, `external_contact`, `unclear_message`,
   `urgent_request`, `service_request`, `reschedule_or_cancel`): the TASK 04/05a placeholders,
   kept as types until the panel decides how staff see them.
3. **`tasks-overdue` is one SQL update**, not entity by entity: the same rule as
   `StaffTask.CheckOverdue`, faster, and alerts come later anyway.
4. **`classify-v5` after v4**: v4 misread self-introductions (lawyer / press / company); v5 was
   written from the 196-sample misses only and checked on the hold-out set, which was not used
   for tuning.
5. **«البوليس جه ومعاه أمر ضبط» is not urgent** for the AI: this follows the owner's decision
   (urgent only when the client says it), although the hold-out label said urgent.
