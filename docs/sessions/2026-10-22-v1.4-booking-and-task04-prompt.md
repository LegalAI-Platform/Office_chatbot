1. Booking with **no human step at all**: no payment confirmation, no staff booking an

2. No payment gateway (Paymob/Fawry…). No cash at all.
3. His rules (pasted by the owner): office hours daily 10:00–24:00, Hazem works 24 h; prices
   (phone/video 15 min 500; office 30 min 1,000; urgent phone 15 min 1,000; document review
   1,500–3,000 priced by him before payment; contract drafting from 3,000; formal notice 2,000;
   Urfi 5,000 with office witnesses / 4,000 with the client's; written opinion = consultation +
   2,000 after the consultation; case representation priced after the consultation, Hazem never
   says it); payment in advance by InstaPay or a cash wallet only; refund rules (full if cancelled
   within 24 h of payment and before the service; office cancels → new slot or full refund; no-show
   → no refund; overpaid → refunded; underpaid → complete it before the service is confirmed).
4. **Decisive clarification:** "the lawyer checks himself, before the appointment, whether the
   transfer arrived — manually."
5. Urfi and special cases: the same way (instant booking, he reviews documents before the appointment).

Resulting design (v1.4): client picks a free slot from the lawyer's Google Calendar (office hours
minus any calendar event) → booking confirmed at once and written to the calendar → payment
details sent → the receipt image is stored (office-sensitive), never reviewed by the system and
never sent to any AI → the calendar event shows only "receipt sent ✅ / not yet ❌" → the lawyer
checks the transfer himself and can mark verified / partial / not received or cancel. Only
**refund transfers** need a person (no API for InstaPay/wallets). A calendar event added on top of
a booking = office cancellation → the client is offered a new slot or a full refund automatically.
