## 4. Differences from the prompt, and why

1. **Tomorrow's unconfirmed count is at the top of «المواعيد»**, not a fifth card on the home page: the home page is the
   owner's mockup with four cards. Its «مواعيد اليوم» card (empty until now) shows today's appointments instead.
2. **The two reminder texts are the bodies submitted to Meta**, not M-03's table as first written (TASK 02 added
   «التفاصيل:» and a closing sentence, because Meta refuses a template ending with a variable). DECISIONS v1.16 quotes them
   in B-18 and updates the M-03 table, so the word-for-word test covers them.
3. **No database trigger:** the worker reconciles the reminders with the appointments every round (made, moved,
   cancelled), so every path that changes an appointment is covered and the timing lives in one tested function.
4. **The two hours reminder is never sent on the day before** (it says «النهارده»), and a reminder reached late goes only
   while its words are still true. B-18 did not say it; it follows from the approved words.
5. **`sending`:** a reminder is claimed just before it goes (from the review, below); one cut off half way is tried
   again after 5 minutes under the same key.

## 4b. The independent review: 6 findings, all fixed

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A reminder read in a batch could go after its appointment was cancelled or moved, or be marked failed when two rounds overlapped | Claimed right before the send (`sending`), only while its appointment is confirmed at that time; a send cut off half way is retried after 5 minutes under the same key |
| 2 | Send keys came from the row id, which a migration rollback reuses (n8n would answer «already sent» for another client) | Keys from the appointment, kind and time; the office's task uses the same key |
| 3 | Re-applying 0017 after a rollback left the texts a reply had used switched off | Switched on again, unless the office reworded one since |
| 4 | «هحضر» pressed while a calendar update was on its way could be lost from the event | The update is marked done only if the row did not change meanwhile; otherwise it runs once more |
| 5 | e2e step 15 read the reminder's state once, before the API could record the send | Waits for it |
| 6 | One test could pass without its hold | Asserts the hold exists |
